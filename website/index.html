A timer for people who lose time

See time pass, not just count down.

TimeSense turns a duration into a shape you can watch shrink. No digits to ignore, no alarm that jolts you, no streaks to break. Just a clearer sense of how long things actually take.

Features

Built around the way time actually goes missing.

Predicted vs. actual

Every timer keeps your guess next to the real number, so your sense of a task corrects itself over weeks.

Six timer styles

Radial, Pizza, Plant, Moon, Monk, Cat. Pick the one your brain keeps looking at, and hide the clock if the count stresses you.

Honest buttons

Pause, +5 min, and Got distracted. Nothing here treats a real day as a failure.

Routines that repeat

Set the days, a reminder time, and whether it ever ends. Today's routines wait for you on the home screen.

Widget, at a glance

Today's routine sits on your home screen — no need to open the app to know what's due.

Insights, not charts

Which tasks you under-call, which ones drag, what time of day works. Plain readings, nothing to decode.

Setting one up

A prediction, a repeat schedule, a style, a category. Then it never needs setting up again.

Closing it out

Two taps at the end — the real duration and how it felt. That's the whole data-entry burden.
