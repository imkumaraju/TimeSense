TimeSense — timer style, Concept D

The moon crosses
the sky, once.

An ambient, low-motion timer style: the moon rises at one horizon when the task starts, arcs overhead, and sets at the opposite horizon exactly when time's up. No shrinking shapes, no countdown pressure — just a single slow arc, which makes it a strong candidate for the reduced-motion accessibility mode. Same warm palette as the rest of the app, used as the moon's glow and the dawn/dusk horizon light rather than as flat page color.

Moon Arc Timer

Same t = elapsed / total value as the other styles — here it maps directly to an angle along a semicircular arc from horizon to horizon.

  • 0% — moonrise, warm horizon glow, task just started
  • ~50% — zenith, deepest night, stars fully out — the "middle of the task" moment
  • 85–100% — moonset, horizon glow returns on the opposite side, signaling time's almost up
