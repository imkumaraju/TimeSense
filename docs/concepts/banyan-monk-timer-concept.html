TimeSense — timer style, Concept G

Sit. Breathe.
Time passes on its own.

A monk walks up to a banyan tree, sits, and closes their eyes as the task begins. A quiet glow behind them deepens the longer they sit — not a countdown, a sense of settling. When the timer ends, their eyes open. Best suited to longer focus sessions (deep work, reading, studying) rather than quick 5-minute tasks, where the slow arrival wouldn't have time to land.

Banyan Tree Meditation

Same t = elapsed / total value as every other style — here it drives the monk's walk-in, then the halo's glow radius, then the moment their eyes open.

  • 0–8% — walks in from the edge of the frame and sits beneath the tree
  • 8–95% — eyes closed, breathing gently, the halo behind them slowly brightens and widens, faint rising motes mark the passage of time
  • 95–100% — eyes open, halo settles into a steady glow — task complete
