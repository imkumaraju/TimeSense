Reading the board: the top row is the linear "happy path" a task goes through (01→05), looping back to Home on save. The bottom row shows the two tab screens reachable anytime from Home's bottom nav — they're not part of the sequence, so they're laid out as siblings rather than numbered steps. This maps directly onto the screens listed in Section 3 of the build spec, in the same order.
