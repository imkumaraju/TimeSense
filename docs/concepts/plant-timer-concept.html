TimeSense — timer style, Concept C

A seed grows,
blooms, and fades.

A third visual timer style alongside the radial and sliced pizza: a plant that sprouts at the start of the task, grows and flowers through the middle, then wilts as time runs out — so "time's almost up" is felt as a flower closing, not just a color turning red. Same warm palette as the rest of the app throughout, so it reads as one family of styles.

Plant Timer

Same underlying elapsed/remaining math as the other two styles — this is purely a different skin driven by the same t = elapsed / total value.

  • 0–50% — stem grows, first leaves unfurl
  • 45–55% — a bud forms at the tip
  • 55–85% — flower opens, full bloom held
  • 85–100% — petals droop and brown, stem bows — task is almost out of time

Consistency check: every screen here reuses the same six tokens as the rest of the spec — crust gold, sauce red, cheese cream, basil green, board dark, and plate cream — so Plant, Radial, and Slices read as three skins of one design system, not three different apps.
