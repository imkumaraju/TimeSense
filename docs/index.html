TimeSense

TimeSense is an ADHD-friendly timer app for iOS and Android. Instead of a plain digital countdown, it shows a bespoke full-screen visual timer (a shrinking pizza, draining bar, growing plant, and more) so remaining time is felt, not just read. It also logs how long tasks actually took against your own prediction, building a personal calibration history over time to help you estimate better.
